STAGE 01 OF 08 · SITE RECCE, LASER SURVEY & OBSTRUCTION AUDIT

Phoenix Citadel Mall Survey + Commercial Wall/Glass Obstruction Recce

Instruments: Bosch GLM 50-27 Laser Disto · Steel Tape · Moisture/Lux Meter

📸 Original Phoenix Citadel Indore Site Survey Archive (All 8 Raw Recce Reference Photos):

1. EXTERIOR, FACADE & HIGH-STREET RECCE

Structure, Wind & Power Audit

  • 3-Point Cavity Check: Measure W × H × Depth at top, center & base to catch civil plaster sag; audit AC outdoor units on clinics/shops for CNC Louver Hiders.
  • Backing & DB Feed: Check RCC vs hollow sandstone/brick for `M10 Fischer` anchors & locate nearest `230V 16A MCB` DB point.
2. INTERIOR SPACE, IN-SHOP & MALL VM RECCE

Obstruction & RDD Compliance

  • Obstruction X-Y Grid: Map switchboards, AC thermostats, fire alarms & `100mm` skirting so NO text/face ever gets cut!
  • Glass & Lift Audit: Lock conference/escalator glass bands (`900–1950mm AFFL`), lift door center-gap (`6mm`), and `3M Primer 94` paint check.
3. EVENT, 3D STALL, FLEET & PACKAGING RECCE

Rigging, Vehicles & Product Spec

  • Event & 3D Stall: Forecourt `No-Drill Counterweight` rule, Atrium `26ft` truss wire hooks, Cargo lift size & `11 PM – 7 AM` night window.
  • Fleet & Packaging: Photograph bus/buggy/SUV dead-flat with `1000mm` ruler taped on body; caliper-measure gift card/bottle (`mm`) for die-line!
